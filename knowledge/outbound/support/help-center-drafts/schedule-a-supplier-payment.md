# How do I schedule a supplier payment for a future date?
**DRAFT help-center article — from 7 identical tickets (6 customers), median 4.3h each. Deflects ~30h/quarter.**

Yes — payments can be scheduled up to 90 days ahead.
1. **Payments → New payment**, enter the beneficiary and amount
2. Under **When**, pick **Schedule for later** and choose the date
3. Confirm. The payment shows as *Scheduled* and funds are reserved the
   morning it executes.
You can cancel or edit any scheduled payment until 22:00 ET the day before.
