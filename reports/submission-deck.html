Q1 · WHAT PROBLEM

Ops runs the old way:
one ticket at a time.

Ops integrates more information than any other role — yet it still works in specialist columns and handoffs. In the dataset, 38 of 100 tickets aren't support work at all; they're cross-ticket problems wearing ticket costumes, and nothing reclaims them:

▲ Burst incident

Card outage, 4 days

14 tickets under 14 different subject lines — never declared as one incident.

▲ Burst incident

$342k wires stuck

9 tickets, 8 customers; payroll and suppliers blocked; 5 of 9 unresolved.

⚠ Slow-burn trend

FX churn signal

10 customers over 3 weeks, CSAT 1.67 — invisible to any daily review.

◆ Hidden risk

"Quick question"

An unrecognized admin — a likely account takeover, still open.

Ops doesn't need a faster version of the old way — it needs an AI-native one.

q3 — how · four learning channels06 / 08
Q3 · HOW — THE TEAM LEARNS, WITH AUTHORITY CHECKED

Four channels. Each with the right authority.

1 · corrections

Ops-role-gated, enforced in code. One correction = override + few-shot + eval case.

2 · coaching

"From now on…" → one rule in NOTES.md. Capped at 20, git-audited, human-pruned.

3 · self-learning

Nightly journals + weekly digests into KNOWLEDGE.md — facts only, never rules. Agents commit under their own git author.

4 · skill evolution

Agents propose frozen diffs; sophie approves in chat; the executor can apply only the recorded diff — humans-only by construction.

q4 — assumptions · team design07 / 08
Q4 · KEY ASSUMPTIONS

The core assumption is a team design.

4 agent roles — execution

Triage

queue, routing, corrections

Pattern Commander

incident & trend cards

Case Worker

playbook work packages

Liaison

cross-team briefs, tracker

3 human roles — judgment (one operator wears all three hats in this demo)

Loop owner

runs the queue, makes the calls

Platform specialist

encodes expertise into playbooks & skills

Loop manager

reviews corrections, approves outbound

The seams where they split at scale: correct_labelsapprove_draftsapprove_escalationsapprove_skill_changes
Also assumed: ticket text is the only system of record · monthly revenue proxies impact · synthetic data → no privacy concern in LLM calls
