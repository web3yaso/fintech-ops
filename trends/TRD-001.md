## Recommendation
**Recommendation (not a directive):** route to Mei (FX pricing owner) for a spread/pricing review. The complaint is consistent (rate vs. market/Google benchmark) across 10 independent accounts and $58k MRR over 18 days with no sign of slowing (2 new tickets still pending as of the last-seen date). Suggest checking whether the current CAD→USD spread is out of line with competitor benchmarks the customers are citing, and whether proactive rate-transparency messaging (e.g. showing the spread at time of conversion) would reduce ticket volume.
